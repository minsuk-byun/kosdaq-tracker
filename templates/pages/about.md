# About
{{SITE_NAME}} publishes short English summaries of disclosures filed by KOSDAQ-listed and smaller KOSPI-listed companies on DART, the electronic disclosure system run by Korea's Financial Supervisory Service.

## Why this site exists

Korea's largest listed companies now publish key disclosures in English. Most small and mid-sized companies do not, so investors who do not read Korean find it hard to follow buybacks, dividends, new share issues, changes of control and large contracts at these companies. This site fills that gap.

## How it works

1. Each day, new filings of six types are collected from DART's official open API.
2. A summary is written in English with the help of an AI model.
3. Every number in the summary is checked automatically against the original filing. If any number does not match, the summary is held back for human review instead of being published.
4. Each page links to the original filing so you can verify it.

The guides on this site are written and reviewed by the site operator.
