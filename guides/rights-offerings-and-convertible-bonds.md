KOSDAQ companies raise money often, and each raise can dilute existing shareholders. Knowing the type of raise tells you who gets the new shares and at what price.

## Four ways to issue new shares (유상증자)

| Korean term | English | Who can buy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 주주배정 | Rights offering | Existing shareholders, in proportion to their holdings |
| 주주배정후 실권주 일반공모 | Rights offering, then public offering of unsubscribed shares | Existing shareholders first, then the public |
| 일반공모 | Public offering | Anyone |
| 제3자배정 | Third-party allotment | Named investors chosen by the company |

Third-party allotments are worth reading closely. The filing names the investors, and the price and lock-up period tell you a lot about the deal.

## The timeline of a rights offering

1. Board decision (이사회결의) with an **expected** issue price.
2. Record date (신주배정기준일): shareholders on this date receive rights.
3. Rights trading period, when rights can be sold on the exchange.
4. Final issue price (확정발행가), usually set shortly before subscription. It can be lower than the expected price if the share price falls.
5. Subscription (청약) and payment (납입).
6. New shares are listed.

## Convertible bonds (전환사채, CB)

A convertible bond is debt that the holder can turn into shares at a set **conversion price** (전환가액). Key terms to check:

- **Coupon and yield to maturity** (표면이자율, 만기이자율). Many small-cap CBs pay 0% coupon.
- **Conversion price and number of shares.** The filing states how many shares full conversion would create and what percentage of total shares that is.
- **Price reset (리픽싱).** Many Korean CBs allow the conversion price to be lowered if the share price falls, down to a stated floor (최저 조정가액). A lower price means more new shares.
- **Conversion window** (전환청구기간). Dilution can only happen once this period starts.
- **Put and call options.** Holders may be able to demand early repayment, and the company or its largest shareholder may have the right to buy bonds back.

Related instruments are bonds with warrants (신주인수권부사채, BW) and exchangeable bonds (교환사채, EB), which convert into shares the company already holds.

## Reading the purpose

The filing lists how the money will be used: 운영자금 (working capital), 시설자금 (capital spending), 채무상환자금 (debt repayment) or 타법인증권취득자금 (buying other companies' shares). Repeated working-capital raises are something many investors look at alongside the company's cash flow.
