DART (Data Analysis, Retrieval and Transfer System) is the electronic disclosure system run by Korea's Financial Supervisory Service. Every listed Korean company files its reports there, and most filings are only in Korean.

## The parts of a filing page

| Part | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Company name (회사명) | The filer. KOSDAQ companies are marked 코 and KOSPI companies 유. |
| Report name (보고서명) | The type of filing, such as 자기주식취득결정 (decision to buy back shares). |
| Filer (제출인) | Usually the company itself. Share-ownership reports are often filed by the shareholder. |
| Receipt date (접수일자) | The date the filing was submitted. |
| Receipt number (접수번호) | A 14-digit ID. The first eight digits are the date. It is the best way to cite a filing. |

## Labels in square brackets

A report name sometimes starts with a label in brackets. The most common ones:

- **[기재정정]** — an amended filing. The company corrected or updated an earlier filing. Read the 정정사유 (reason for correction) section first.
- **[첨부정정]** — the attachments were corrected, not the main text.
- **[발행조건확정]** — the final terms of a share or bond issue have been set (for example, the final issue price).
- **(자율공시)** — a voluntary disclosure that the rules did not strictly require.

## Major reports vs exchange disclosures

Many important events appear as **주요사항보고서** (major matters reports), which are filed under the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act. Others, such as dividend decisions and large contracts, are **exchange disclosures** (거래소공시) filed under Korea Exchange rules. Both appear on DART.

## Reading the numbers

- 원 is Korean won. 억 is 100 million and 조 is 1 trillion, so 50억원 is KRW 5 billion.
- 주 means shares. 비율(%) is a percentage.
- Dates are usually written as 2026년 11월 03일 or 2026-11-03.

## A quick workflow

1. Check the report name and any bracket label.
2. Find the decision date (이사회결의일 or 결정일) and the key amounts.
3. For amended filings, compare with the earlier version.
4. Use a browser translator for the free-text sections, but rely on the numbers in the tables.

Every summary on this site links to the original filing on DART.
